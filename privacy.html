HealthPilotAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026 · For closed testing and Play / App Store listings

Who we are

HealthPilotAI (“we”, “the app”) provides general wellness and meal-logging insights. Contact: use the support email shown in the app (Profile → About & privacy) or in the store listing.

What we collect

  • Account: email (and Google account identifiers if you use Google Sign-In).
  • Profile: name, age, sex/gender, height, weight, goals, conditions, medications, allergies, blood pressure notes, exercise level (as you enter them).
  • Meals: descriptions, optional photos, AI-generated scores and suggestions.
  • Labs: values you manually enter (e.g. cholesterol, triglycerides).
  • Technical: device/app diagnostics if crash reporting is enabled later.

How we use data

  • To personalise wellness tips and health scores from your logs.
  • To operate authentication, sync (Firebase), and meal analysis (API / AI providers).
  • We do not sell your personal health data.

AI processing

Meal text/photos may be sent to an AI provider to generate scores and suggestions. Do not upload information you are not comfortable sharing with that processing.

Important notice

HealthPilotAI is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified clinician for health decisions.

Retention and deletion

You can Delete account in Profile. That removes your Firebase Auth account and associated profile, meals, labs, and meal photos we store for your user id, subject to normal backup lag.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Use in-app delete or contact support.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after posting means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Use the support email shown in the app Profile → About & privacy.
